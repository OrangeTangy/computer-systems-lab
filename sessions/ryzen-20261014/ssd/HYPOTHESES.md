# Predictions before the SSD follow-up

Increasing random-read QD should initially raise IOPS, then add latency with smaller throughput gains. The operational knee is the first sampled QD reaching 90% of the within-session maximum median; uncertainty and non-monotonicity may make it ambiguous. p99.9 may vary strongly with short trials. One interleaved session avoids the historical QD128/256 session boundary, but cannot eliminate system-drive interference or establish steady state.
